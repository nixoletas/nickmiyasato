My first project in Next.js, built for a private security company that needed a credible web presence and had nothing.

Security services sell on trust, so the page leans on clarity over cleverness: what the company does, how to reach them, and nothing competing for attention with those two things.

It was also where server-side rendering stopped being an abstract concept for me. A small marketing site is a good place to learn what SSR actually buys you, because the answer — a page that is complete and indexable on first response — is directly the thing the client is paying for.

It has since grown past the single page it started as, covering the three sides of the business separately: guarding and escort, the technology side of CCTV and monitored alarms and access control, and the facilities work that runs alongside them. It now serves the company's own domain at [famsecurity.com.br](https://www.famsecurity.com.br).
